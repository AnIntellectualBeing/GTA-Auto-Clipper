Privacy notice

Checkout

Razorpay processes payment details during checkout. This site never receives or stores your full card or UPI credentials. The ₹149 guide checkout does not ask for an email address; the MasterClip checkout does (see below). Razorpay may request contact details as part of its checkout flow.

Guide access

After payment verification, this site stores a short-lived, secure browser cookie so you can download the guide. The cookie contains no card or payment credentials and expires after 24 hours.

MasterClip delivery

The MasterClip checkout asks for your email address (and optionally your phone number). They are passed to Razorpay with the order so your purchase can be found again if you lose your link, and, if email delivery is switched on, used to email you your access link through our email provider (Resend). After payment you get a signed access link valid for about a year; keep it private. The browser may remember the order reference and access link in local storage. The recovery service checks the payment ID, order, email or phone with Razorpay before issuing a link.

Clipper software

The open-source clipper runs on your computer. It uses the YouTube Data API for stream discovery when configured and yt-dlp/FFmpeg to process a selected stream or local file. The software does not send rendered clips to this website.
