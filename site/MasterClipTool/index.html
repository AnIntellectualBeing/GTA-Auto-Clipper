Then it runs by itself.

STEP 1

Find a stream

Type a game or a streamer. Pick a live stream, a past stream, paste a link, or use a video file.

  • YouTube and Twitch
  • Past streams: take a short part, it's much faster
STEP 2

Pick a look

Streamer cam on top and gameplay below, big captions, meme bars and more. Drag anything to move it.

  • Word-by-word captions
  • Hinglish for Hindi streams
STEP 3

Get your clips

MasterClip finds the hype moments and cuts them. Edit, download, or post each one, or join your favourites into a YouTube compilation.

  • Short 9:16, long 16:9, or both
  • Speed depends on your PC
STEP 4

Let it post

Connect a free Zernio account once. Every new clip goes to your Instagram and YouTube accounts at the pace you set. Add more Zernio accounts with + Add.

  • Clips per hour you choose
  • Spread across accounts
